# Sweet Dream Pastry Shop: customer policies (fictional demo data)

This document is invented for a learning project. It describes a made-up pastry shop and is not a real business.

## Orders and lead time
Custom cakes must be ordered at least 3 days in advance. Standard cakes from the display case can be bought the same day while stock lasts. Orders for more than 4 kg of cake or for weddings require at least 14 days of notice and a consultation call.

## Prices
A standard Napoleon cake costs 450 CZK per kilogram. A chocolate cake costs 520 CZK per kilogram. Custom decoration (photo print, figurines) is charged separately, starting from 150 CZK. Cupcakes are sold in boxes of 6 for 240 CZK.

## Payment and deposit
Custom orders require a 30% deposit when the order is confirmed. The remaining amount is paid on pickup or delivery. We accept cards and cash. We do not accept cryptocurrency.

## Cancellations and changes
A custom order can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before pickup, and the deposit is refunded in full. Cancellations inside 48 hours forfeit the deposit because the ingredients are already purchased. The design of a cake can be changed up to 72 hours before pickup.

## Delivery
We deliver within Hradec Kralove city limits for 120 CZK. Delivery outside the city is not available. Delivery slots are between 9:00 and 18:00 on weekdays and between 9:00 and 14:00 on Saturdays. We do not deliver on Sundays.

## Opening hours
The shop is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00, Saturday from 8:00 to 14:00. The shop is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

## Allergens and dietary options
Most cakes contain gluten, eggs, milk and nuts. We offer a gluten-free cake on request with 5 days of notice, but it is prepared in the same kitchen, so traces of gluten are possible. We cannot guarantee a nut-free product. Vegan cakes are not offered.

## Storage and freshness
Cakes with cream should be kept in a refrigerator at 2 to 6 degrees Celsius and eaten within 3 days. Cakes can be frozen for up to 1 month, but decorations may change after thawing.

## Complaints and refunds
If a cake does not match the confirmed order, please send a photo within 24 hours of pickup to the shop email. We will offer a replacement or a refund after reviewing the photo. Complaints about taste preference alone are handled case by case.
